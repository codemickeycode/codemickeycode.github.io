# Blog-Informed Content Direction

## Decision

The new website should use Micaela's blog as a source of voice, stories, and themes rather than presenting a generic personal-brand narrative.

The visual direction remains **Editorial Minimalism with Industrial Warmth**, but the content should become more specific, candid, and recognizably hers.

## Voice

The website should feel:

- Conversational and direct
- Candid about uncertainty, burnout, ambition, and growth
- Warm without becoming motivational or polished
- Curious, occasionally funny, and willing to show strong opinions
- Grounded in stories, people, and lived experience

Avoid vague phrases such as “questions worth staying with” or “a corner for curiosity” when a concrete personal story can do the work instead.

## Narrative Threads

The site content should connect three recurring parts of Micaela's identity:

### Building

Software engineering, backend development, product thinking, program management, and the systems that help ideas become useful in the real world.

### Belonging

PythonPH, PyLadies Manila, mentorship, volunteer leadership, community sustainability, and creating opportunities for people to grow.

### Becoming

Career changes, taking dreams seriously, developing inner strength, recovering from burnout, making room for freedom, and continuing to change.

## Homepage Content Direction

The homepage should answer these questions with specific language:

- Who is Micaela?
- What does she build and enable?
- What does she care about?
- What has she learned from doing the work?

Suggested positioning:

> I started as a software engineer because I liked building things and understanding how they worked. Over time, I became just as interested in the people, decisions, and systems around the software.

Suggested connective statement:

> Code gave me a way to build. Community gave me people to build with. Curiosity keeps me asking what the work is for.

## Content Principles

- Prefer specific stories over broad categories.
- Show evidence and context instead of only listing accomplishments.
- Describe personal interests through real experiences, not lifestyle labels.
- Use the blog's natural voice, including appropriate humor and imperfection.
- Keep sensitive or intimate blog material private unless Micaela explicitly chooses to publish it on the website.
- Let recognition point back to the people and systems that made the work possible.
- Use fewer, stronger stories instead of filling every section with placeholder content.

## Source Material

The following blog themes are especially useful:

- The PSF Community Service Award and the work of building systems that outlast founders
- Combining technical skills with entrepreneurial and product thinking
- Women developing inner strength and agency
- Career retrospectives, hackathons, travel, hobbies, and recovery from burnout
- The people and friendships that made community work sustainable

The current root `index.html` remains the primary source for professional facts. The blog is a source for voice, narrative, and supporting context. Archived site versions should only be used when they fill a clear content gap.

## Intentional Living

Micaela's memento mori mindset should inform the website's purpose and pacing. Life is finite, so attention, work, relationships, and choices should matter.

This principle should remain an underlying philosophy rather than a repeated phrase or a dark visual motif.

### Core Statement

> Because life is finite, I want to spend it intentionally: building useful things, investing in people, staying curious, and making room for experiences that matter.

### Design Implications

- Respect visitors' limited time through clarity and straightforward navigation.
- Create a calm pace rather than urgency-driven interaction.
- Prioritize meaningful stories over an exhaustive portfolio.
- Present accomplishments with context and gratitude rather than self-promotion.
- Include retrospectives, lessons, transitions, and changes in direction.
- Treat photographs as memories and observations, not decorative lifestyle imagery.
- Let the site evolve as a record of a life in progress.
- Keep the interface simple because attention is valuable.
- Avoid literal death imagery, clocks, skulls, or overly dramatic symbolism.

### Narrative Connection

The three narrative threads can be understood through intentional living:

- **Building:** What I choose to make with my limited time.
- **Belonging:** The people and communities I choose to invest in.
- **Becoming:** How I keep changing, learning, and deciding what matters.

Suggested connective statement:

> Life is short, so I want to be deliberate about what I build, who I build with, and what I give my attention to.
