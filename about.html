About
Micaela

I started with software. I stayed for the people, the problems behind the problems, and the possibility that useful things can make life a little more navigable.

The short version

still becoming

I build systems that align tools and technology with human needs. My work has moved from writing software to helping ideas become programs, communities, and institutions that can keep working in the real world.

As Program Coordinator of Ateneo TRL (Technical Resource Lab), I help shape efforts around digital public infrastructure, building open, people-centered systems that make government services more inclusive and accessible.

I’m also a Co-founder and Board of Trustee at Python Philippines. After serving as Director of Operations, I now support the organization through governance, institutional continuity, and ecosystem building.

It took me time to see business sense, persistence, and an instinct for bringing people together as part of my technical journey. Now I see them as connected: it matters what we build, but it also matters why it should exist, who it serves, and whether it can survive.

I try to be deliberate about where my time goes. That means choosing work that is useful, showing up for people, and leaving enough room to keep learning and notice a life outside of achievement.
