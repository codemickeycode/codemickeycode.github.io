About
Micaela

I started as a software engineer because I liked building things and understanding how they worked. Over time, I became just as interested in the people, decisions, and systems around the software.

The short version

still becoming

I build systems that align tools and technology with human needs.

My work has moved from writing software to developing programs, communities, and institutions that can keep working beyond the initial idea. I care about whether something is useful, whether people can adopt it, and whether it can continue without depending on one person.

As Program Coordinator of Ateneo TRL (Technical Resource Lab), I help shape efforts around digital public infrastructure, building open, people-centered systems that make government services more inclusive and accessible.

I’m also a Co-founder and Board of Trustee at Python Philippines. After serving as Director of Operations, I now support the organization through governance, institutional continuity, and ecosystem building.

How I work

Useful work,
built to last.

I try to connect technical possibility with the people, decisions, and systems that make an idea work in practice.

Start with the people and the problem

Technology is only useful when it responds to something real. I try to understand the people affected, the decisions they need to make, and the constraints they are working within.

Make complexity usable

I enjoy making technical, organizational, and strategic problems easier to understand so people can make better decisions and take meaningful action.

Build for continuity

The strongest work does not end when the first builder steps away. I care about documentation, shared ownership, good systems, and foundations that help others continue the work.
