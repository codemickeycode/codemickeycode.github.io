A note on this space

no star ratings here

Little Discoveries is a place for things that genuinely caught my attention. A coffee shop worth remembering, a local brand found at a fair, a book that sparked a question, or a travel experience that changed the way I looked at something.

These are observations, not recommendations or rankings. The point is to share what made an experience meaningful and give someone else a small path toward curiosity.
