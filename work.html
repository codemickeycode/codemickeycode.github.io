Featured initiative · PythonPH

PythonPH Kaizend

For more than a decade, PythonPH grew through events, mentoring, and volunteers figuring things out as they went. Kaizend became a space where people could learn by doing, take on real responsibility, and grow into organizers and community leaders.

Some of those volunteers now run PythonPH themselves. That is the part of the work I am proudest of: building something that can continue beyond the people who started it.
