About
Micaela

Tech Ecosystem Builder with Software Engineering Background

Nearly two decades across software engineering, program management, and community building. Currently, Program Coordinator for Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) at Ateneo’s Technical Resource Lab. Co-founder of Python Philippines (PythonPH), former Director of Operations, Python Software Foundation (PSF) Fellow and Community Service Awardee.

The long version

My work has moved from writing software to developing programs, communities, and institutions that can keep working beyond the initial idea. I care about whether something is useful, whether people can adopt it, and whether it can continue without depending on one person.

I started as a backend and web developer, building telco revenue systems and reporting tools at Smart Communications, and growth and marketplace features at Freelancer.com. At Complete Business Online, I led a centralized event logging and notification system across the company’s SaaS products on AWS. I also led a Xero integration that Adobe Business Catalyst named its “App of the Month.”

As Program Coordinator at Ateneo’s Technical Resource Lab (TRL), I lead and coordinate initiatives to accelerate Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) adoption in the Philippines, particularly in digital identity, payments, and social protection. My work includes technical capacity building and engaging financial institutions and government agencies to advance National ID adoption, with the goal of building open, people-centered systems that make government services more inclusive and accessible.

I’m also a co-founder of Python Philippines (PythonPH) and a member of its Board of Trustees. I previously served as its Director of Operations from 2018 to 2026. In that role, I helped fund PythonAsia 2026 through sponsorships and partnerships despite a pause in PSF grants, while training new volunteer leads to run sponsorships themselves. Today I support the organization’s governance and institutional continuity, and focus on leadership development and technical learning for core volunteers.
